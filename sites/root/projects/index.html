LAB STATUS PIPELINE 2026 · LIVE
lab.monk97.me

SPLITSHEET 2026 · MIT
Splitwise, but it's a Google Sheet
  • A shared-expense tracker built in Google Sheets with Apps Script: equal, exact, percentage and share splits, recurring monthly expenses, and a single net balance per person.
  • One rule holds it together: the Expenses tab is the only source of truth. The ledger is rebuilt from it and every balance is a live formula, so one refresh recomputes the whole system and nothing can drift.
  • Suggests the smallest set of payments that settles everyone up.
  • Runs on infrastructure people already have, instead of a new stack to host. Built with Claude. source ↗
